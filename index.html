About Me

I'm Chirag Salvi, a Professional Martial Arts Coach and Self-Defense Educator with over 12 years of experience in training individuals, corporates, and institutions in real-world safety, combat readiness, and performance conditioning.

As the Founder of Battle Fit & Battle Fit Academy, I have successfully conducted more than 600+ self-defense workshops and awareness programs across the corporate, educational, and NGO sectors, empowering over 1,000+ professionals, students, and athletes.

My teaching approach blends Judo, Krav Maga, Kickboxing, Kalaripayattu, and Functional Fitness, focusing on awareness, adaptability, and control.
